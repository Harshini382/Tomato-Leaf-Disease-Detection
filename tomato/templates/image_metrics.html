Metrics Explanation

📈 PSNR (Peak Signal-to-Noise Ratio)

Measures the quality of the processed image compared to the original. Higher values (>30dB) indicate better quality. PSNR=∞ means identical images.

🎲 Entropy

Measures the amount of information/randomness in the image. Higher entropy (max ~8 for 8-bit images) means more varied pixel intensities. Uniform images have low entropy.

🎨 Contrast Index

Measures the variation in pixel intensities (RMS contrast). Higher values indicate more contrast between bright and dark areas. Range: 0-1 (normalized).

📊 Histogram Spread

Analyzes how pixel values are distributed across the intensity range. Shows the shape, spread, and statistical properties of the histogram distribution.
