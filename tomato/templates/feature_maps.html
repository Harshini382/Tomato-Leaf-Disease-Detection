CNN Feature Maps

What are Feature Maps?

Feature maps are learned representations from the CNN layers. Each filter (neuron) in a layer learns to detect different patterns in the image such as edges, textures, and shapes. The visualization shows the 12 most important feature maps extracted from the second-to-last layer of the model.

Grayscale Histogram

Understanding the Histogram
  • Left chart: Shows the frequency distribution of pixel intensities (0-255)
  • Right chart: Cumulative distribution showing what percentage of pixels have intensity below each value
  • High spread: Indicates good contrast in the image
  • Narrow peaks: Indicates limited color (brightness) variation

RGB Channel Histogram

RGB Histogram Analysis

This histogram shows the distribution of pixel intensities in each color channel (Red, Green, Blue) separately. Overlapping distributions indicate balanced color channels, while separated distributions suggest color dominance in the image. This is useful for understanding the color composition and detecting color abnormalities in leaf images.
